About the project

OneDayOneEvent is an independent, operator-run project producing short-form educational and informational media about historical events, scientific achievements, technology, culture, exploration, and other notable events associated with dates of the year.

The current service and API client are operated only for the project’s own content and channel. This is not a consumer account-linking service, and external users cannot connect their own Google or YouTube accounts.
